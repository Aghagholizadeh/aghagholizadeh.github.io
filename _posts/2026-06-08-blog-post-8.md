<p>I am pleased to share our recent publication in <i>Earthquake Engineering & Structural Dynamics</i>:</p>

<p> <b>"Weak-Story Mitigation and Drift Redistribution in Moment-Resisting Frames"</b><br><br>

In this study, we investigate strategies for mitigating weak-story behavior and improving the distribution of story drifts in moment-resisting frames. The work examines how structural modifications can redistribute seismic demands and reduce localized drift concentrations, with the goal of improving the overall seismic performance and resilience of the structural system.

</p>
